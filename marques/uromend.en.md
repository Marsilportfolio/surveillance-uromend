Uromend: **133 active ads** (120 on the Uromend page, 13 on "My Bladder And Prostate"), all launched between July and October 2026, 7 videos out of 29 collected ads. Single message "6-in-1 formula", landing pages [uromend.com/pages/v3](https://uromend.com/pages/v3) (17 ads) and v2 (8 ads).

## Hooks, awareness, personas, enemies, narrative (indicative figures from 10/17; the charts below are recalculated at each snapshot)
- **#1 hook: the symptom question** (62%: "Constant urges. Weak flow. Leaks.", "Tired of interrupted sleep…"), then the catalog (28%: "There's a NEW way to treat your prostate! ✅"). In video: "This is 600 mg of pumpkin seed. And this is 600 mg…" (dose comparison), "Do not buy Uromend", "12 years of a weak trickle gone in just 10 days", "This is what would happen to a man on Flomax…".
- **Awareness level**: we speak to the **product aware / most aware** (93%: brand name in the first line, 30% off promo, subscription). Almost nothing for the problem aware or the unaware: that's where competitors recruit.
- **Persona**: 100% man 50+ prostate. No wife, no kidneys, no women.
- **Enemy**: the drug (52%, mostly in video), "what they hide from you" (10%); 38% with no enemy.
- **Narrative**: catalog (24%), 4-week mechanism (7%, the 2 Flomax videos), testimonial (7%, "F*ck this product").

## What's missing compared with Everly / Elivora / Noverly / Purely
- **Flomax is there, but only in video** (2 videos out of 7: "This is what would happen to a man who's been taking Flomax for years if he started taking Uromend daily for four weeks… no dizziness when he gets out of bed", "a 55 year old man on Flomax… without the brain fog from his old pills") and in the "F*ck this product" story (Foley catheter, retention). In the ad **copy**, the word appears only 2 times out of 29. Elivora puts it in 95% of its ads, in the first line.
- **No female narrator**: at competitors, the wife or the nurse tells the story (40% of Elivora ads start with "My husband…"). At Uromend, the brand or the man himself speaks.
- **No concrete fear** (catheter, surgery, the 3 a.m. fall): competitors live on it.
- **Volume**: 133 ads vs 1,340 (Elivora) and 9,300 (Everly). Rankings are won with volume: 3 to 5 new creatives per week minimum.
- **No "media" page** (like Purely's "Top 10 Holistic Tips") to make ads look like articles.
