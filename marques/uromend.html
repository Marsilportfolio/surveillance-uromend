I am 62, and my problems really started creeping up around the time I turned 55 .
It happened slowly at first, but by the time I hit 60 my entire world had shrunk to the radius of the nearest available restroom .
It became the only thing I thought about on my drive home, and I found myself constantly mapping out public bathrooms everywhere I went .
The worst part was lying in bed at 2 AM . I would be utterly exhausted but wide awake, just waiting for the sudden and embarrassing urge to hit me all over again .
When I finally went to the doctor, he simply handed me a prescription for tamsulosin, which is also known as Flomax, and moved right along to his next patient .
The pills certainly helped my stream, but the man taking those pills no longer felt like me .

When I finally decided to look closely at the Supplement Facts panel, I saw the words Proprietary Blend and decided to look up what that actually meant .
I learned that it is a legal loophole that manufacturers use to hide weak, under-dosed formulas . This particular bottle was mostly just standard Saw Palmetto .
According to landmark clinical trials, Saw Palmetto provides little to no significant benefit over a placebo for symptom reduction when it is used completely on its own .
