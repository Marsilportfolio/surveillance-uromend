# STĒLO vs Uromend: revenue estimate (10/19/2026)
Same method for both, same assumptions, so the comparison is fair. Two notions: the **annual run rate** (what the brand would make over 12 months at today's pace) and **revenue collected** (what it actually brought in since launch, accounting for the ramp-up). Saad knows Uromend's real numbers: if they differ from the estimate, the same gap applies to STĒLO (that's how to calibrate the model).

## 2. Shared assumptions
- Visit → order conversion: 1.5% (low), 2.2% (center), 3% (high).
- Average order value: STĒLO $85-100 (3 bottles "most popular" at $110-150); Uromend **$95-120** (packs $129/$199/$349 pushed, higher basket).
- Ad spend ≈ proportional to active ads: $300-750 per active ad per month (range observed for this niche), ROAS 1.5 to 2.5.

## 3. STĒLO
- **From traffic**: 30-80K visits × 1.5-3% × $85-100 = $38K to $240K/month; center 55K × 2.2% × $90 ≈ **$110K/month**.
- **From ads**: 200 ads × $300-750 = $60-150K of ad spend × ROAS 1.5-2.5 = $90-375K; center ≈ **$200K/month**.
- **Current pace**: $90-240K/month, **center ≈ $150K/month → $1.8M per year** (range $1.1-2.9M).
- **Revenue collected since launch** (13 months, with ramp-up: ≈ $20K/month from Oct 2025 to Jan 2026, $50K Feb-Apr, $100K May-Aug when the winning ads ran, $150K Sept-Oct): **≈ $0.9 to 1.2M collected** (center ≈ $1M). Calendar year 2026 (Jan-Dec) at the current pace: ≈ $1.4-1.6M.
- **MRR (subscription)**: 15-30% of revenue → $20-60K/month, center $35K.
- **What they really make (margin)**: product ≈ $8 cost for a $60 price, but ads = 40-60% of revenue in the acquisition phase, plus influencers, refunds (90-day "one email" guarantee) and Shopify/payment fees ≈ 10%. Probable net margin **0 to 15%**, i.e. $0 to $25K/month: they reinvest everything in ads (standard at this stage). The real value created is the customer base and the subscribers.

## 4. Uromend
- **From traffic**: 38K × 1.5-3% × $95-120 = $54K to $137K/month; center 38K × 2.2% × $105 ≈ **$88K/month**.
- **From ads**: 140 ads × $300-750 = $42-105K of ad spend × ROAS 1.5-2.5 = $63-260K; center ≈ **$120K/month**.
- **Current pace**: $60-140K/month, **center ≈ $95K/month → ≈ $1.1M per year** (range $0.7-1.7M). Nitro+ (since July 2026) is included in traffic but not yet in the counted ads.
- **Revenue collected since March 2025** (19 months; slower ramp: ≈ $15K/month until summer 2025, $30-50K late 2025-early 2026, $60-90K since summer 2026 with the ad relaunch in late July): **≈ $0.6 to 0.9M collected** (center ≈ $0.75M).
- **MRR**: 10-25% of revenue (subscription pushed less than at STĒLO) → $10-25K/month.

## 5. Verdict
| | STĒLO | Uromend |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly pace (center) | ≈ $150K | ≈ $95K |
| Annual run rate | ≈ $1.8M | ≈ $1.1M |
| Collected since launch | ≈ $1.0M in 13 months | ≈ $0.75M in 19 months |
| Category | small DTC brand, accelerating fast | small DTC brand, accelerating (+71% traffic) |
| Gap | STĒLO ≈ 1.5 × Uromend today | catchable: same order of magnitude, 60 ads apart |

Market benchmarks: under $5M/year = small brand; $5-20M = mid-size (Elivora is probably there); over $20M = big (Everly). STĒLO and Uromend are both "small", and STĒLO got a 6-month lead with **a single angle** ("they never worked") found in May 2026.

## 6. What could flip the gap
- STĒLO has 1 Facebook page and 1 product: an ad rejection by Meta or a complaint (fake "3,167" reviews, the Uromend bottle in its ad) stops it cold.
- Uromend has 2 products, 2 pages, faster traffic growth (+71% vs +52%) and a better Similarweb rank: the base is healthier; what's missing is ad volume (140 vs 200) and an angle as sharp as theirs. The "Equol War" batch and the "failed solutions" strategy are exactly that lever.

## 7. To calibrate (Saad)
If Uromend's real monthly revenue is known, divide it by $95K: that ratio applies to STĒLO (e.g. if Uromend actually makes $60K/month, ratio 0.63 → STĒLO ≈ $95K/month, ≈ $1.1M/year).
