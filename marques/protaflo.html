Protaflo (prostatediscovery.com) — fiche du 25 octobre 2026

Lu : 32 pubs (texte complet) de la page Protaflo, 22 vidéos, 10 hooks transcrits. Nouvel annonceur apparu le 15 octobre 2026.

En une phrase

Protaflo vend un complément prostate par une longue vidéo de vente (VSL = « video sales letter », 3 à 5 minutes) qui dit que le saw palmetto, le café et la chirurgie ne marchent pas, et que le vrai problème est un « microbe » dans la prostate à nettoyer. 300 pubs le 23 octobre, 350 le 25 octobre (+17 % en 2 jours).

Angles marketing (accroches citées)

  • Le démenti en rafale (hook n°1, 6 vidéos sur 10) : « Best way to shrink a swollen prostate? Cutting out coffee? Nope. Saw palmetto? Nope. Surgery? Definitely not. Here's what actually works. » Trois fausses solutions balayées en 5 secondes, puis la promesse.
  • Le contre-pied : « Alcohol is bad for your prostate, right? Wrong. Here's how to shrink your prostate fast. » Une idée reçue retournée pour arrêter le pouce.
  • Le partage par l'épouse : « People keep sending me this video saying it has the fastest way to shrink your husband's swollen prostate. Let's see. » (la pub la mieux classée, 14 variantes).
  • Le secret que le médecin cache : « I'm going to show you something about your prostate that no doctor ever explains. And it's the real reason nothing you've tried has worked. »
  • La liste de symptômes (texte de toutes les pubs) : « A weak stream… Constant nighttime trips… The inability to urinate… Embarrassing dribbles… An enlarged prostate… And even intimacy issues… Are all symptoms of a much bigger PROSTATE problem. »

Cible

  • Hommes de 50 ans et plus qui se lèvent « 3, 4, 5 fois par nuit », n'arrivent pas à vider leur vessie, et ont déjà essayé le saw palmetto.
  • Qui raconte : un homme (voix off masculine, ton « on nous ment »), plus une variante où la femme partage la vidéo (« your husband's swollen prostate »).
  • Niveau de conscience : conscient du problème et déçu des solutions (il connaît le saw palmetto et le Flomax, il cherche autre chose). C'est exactement le client qu'Uromend vise, mais Protaflo lui dit que la formule d'Uromend « ne marche pas ».

Promesses

  • « Shrink your prostate fast », « do THIS twice a day to shrink prostate naturally » (titre de la page de vente).
  • Une méthode en 3 étapes : 1️⃣ « Cleanse harmful microbes » 2️⃣ « Reduce inflammation and swelling » 3️⃣ « Restore balance to the prostate microbiome ».
  • Résultats annoncés : « Stronger urinary flow • Fewer nighttime bathroom trips • Better sleep and energy • Better stamina in the bedroom ».
  • Garantie 90 jours satisfait ou remboursé. Approuvé « par un médecin » (« Doctor-approved method »), sans nom.
  • Mot magique : le microbiome de la prostate (= les bactéries qui vivent dans la prostate). Réel en science, mais ici c'est l'« ennemi caché » qui explique pourquoi « rien n'a marché ».

In one sentence

Protaflo sells a prostate supplement through a long video sales letter (VSL, 3 to 5 minutes) saying that saw palmetto, coffee and surgery don't work, and that the real problem is a "microbe" inside the prostate that must be cleaned out. 300 ads on 23 October, 350 on 25 October (+17% in 2 days).

Marketing angles (hooks quoted)

  • The rapid-fire denial (hook #1, 6 videos out of 10): "Best way to shrink a swollen prostate? Cutting out coffee? Nope. Saw palmetto? Nope. Surgery? Definitely not. Here's what actually works." Three false solutions swept away in 5 seconds, then the promise.
  • The reversal: "Alcohol is bad for your prostate, right? Wrong. Here's how to shrink your prostate fast." A common belief flipped to stop the thumb.
  • The wife who shares: "People keep sending me this video saying it has the fastest way to shrink your husband's swollen prostate. Let's see." (their best-ranked ad, 14 variants).
  • The secret the doctor hides: "I'm going to show you something about your prostate that no doctor ever explains. And it's the real reason nothing you've tried has worked."
  • The symptom list (text of every ad): "A weak stream… Constant nighttime trips… The inability to urinate… Embarrassing dribbles… An enlarged prostate… And even intimacy issues… Are all symptoms of a much bigger PROSTATE problem."

Target

  • Men 50+ who get up "3, 4, 5 times a night", can't empty their bladder, and have already tried saw palmetto.
  • Who tells the story: a man (male voice-over, "we're being lied to" tone), plus a variant where the wife shares the video ("your husband's swollen prostate").
  • Awareness level: problem-aware and disappointed by the solutions (he knows saw palmetto and Flomax, he's looking for something else). Exactly the customer Uromend targets, but Protaflo tells him Uromend's formula "doesn't work".

Promises

  • "Shrink your prostate fast", "do THIS twice a day to shrink prostate naturally" (sales page headline).
  • A 3-step method: 1️⃣ "Cleanse harmful microbes" 2️⃣ "Reduce inflammation and swelling" 3️⃣ "Restore balance to the prostate microbiome".
  • Announced results: "Stronger urinary flow • Fewer nighttime bathroom trips • Better sleep and energy • Better stamina in the bedroom".
  • 90-day money-back guarantee. "Doctor-approved method", no name given.
  • Magic word: the prostate microbiome (= the bacteria living in the prostate). Real in science, but here it is the "hidden enemy" that explains why "nothing has worked".

What it means for Uromend
