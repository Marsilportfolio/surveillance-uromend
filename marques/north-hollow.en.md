# North Hollow (brand sheet written on November 15, 2026)

## In one sentence
A newcomer (Facebook page "Healthy Living After 55", site north-hollow.com) selling a dry capsule with 8 plant extracts, almost the same formula family as Uromend, by copying the best mechanics of the big players: Elivora's repentant urologist, Everly's "STOP taking prostate supplements" and Purely Nutrient's "journey of the capsule".

## Marketing angles (hooks quoted)
- **The urologist against Tamsulosin** (the generic name of Flomax): "If you are on Tamsulosin and still getting up 2-3 times a night WATCH THIS NOW. Tamsulosin does not heal your prostate. It is your one-way ticket to a catheter and an operation." With a number: "A 2018 European study found that 57 percent of men on Tamsulosin for 5 to 10 years ended up needing surgery anyway." ([see](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1599273474853172), 2 variants, launched November 11).
- **The journey of the capsule, hour by hour**: "Here's what one capsule of North Hollow does inside your prostate, starting the second you swallow it… Hour 2… Hour 12… Hour 24… Week 3" ([see](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1781786059710877)). Key line: "It's not aging. It's compression."
- **"Please STOP taking prostate supplements"**: a word-for-word copy of Everly's ad (Dr. Anastasia Chamberlen), used here to sell a full formula against "cheap saw palmetto powder" ([see](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1132505929144788)).
- **Fear of surgery**: "I'm 58 and I had prostate surgery 18 months ago. Three weeks later my wife found me crying in the garage." (TURP = part of the prostate is removed through the urethra; [see](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=4185278018445661)).
- **The wife telling the story**: "My husband started taking Flomax on March 8th, 2024… He still got up five times a night… your prostate is 74 grams" ([see](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1523728689805625)).

## Target (age, sex, pain, who tells the story)
Men aged 55 to 75 already on Tamsulosin / Flomax, getting up 2 to 5 times a night, whose urologist is starting to mention surgery. Storytellers: a urologist "with 25 years of practice", a 58-year-old man who had surgery, a wife. The page is called "Healthy Living After 55": the age is in the name.

## Promises
Fewer night trips, stronger stream, a better-emptied bladder, deeper sleep. "Triple-Action: Hormone Balance | Healthy Blood Flow | Cellular Cleanse". 30-day guarantee.

## Formats
40 ads read, 16 of them videos. Very long copy (one-page stories) + explainer videos. The account switches on and off: 28 ads on November 7, 0 on the 9th, 13 on November 15 (12 new ones launched between the 11th and the 14th). This is an account in test phase, not yet a machine.

## What it means for Uromend
- This is the closest competitor to us by product: dry capsule, several plants, doses on the label. Their argument "8 ingredients, every dose on the label" is exactly ours (6 ingredients, 1,400 mg). But they only have 320 mg of saw palmetto and do not state the pumpkin dose: our 600 mg of pumpkin seed is a real point of comparison.
- The public restroom hook ("two guys walked in after me…") is very visual and easy to shoot. Keep it in the idea list.
- Small for now (13 ads). To watch: if it passes 50 ads and holds for two snapshots in a row, one of these ads has taken off.
